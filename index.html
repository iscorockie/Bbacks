Building Trust, One Project at a Time

Founded in 2008 in Kampala, Brunchbacks Construction Company has grown from a small local builder into one of Uganda's most trusted construction firms. We specialize in residential, commercial, and infrastructure development, bringing international standards to every project we undertake.

Our team of engineers, architects, and skilled craftsmen work together to deliver projects on time and within budget — without ever compromising on quality or safety.

  • Fully licensed & insured construction company
  • ISO-certified quality management systems
  • Local expertise with international standards
  • Commitment to safety & sustainability
  • Transparent pricing and project timelines

Residential Construction

Commercial Building
